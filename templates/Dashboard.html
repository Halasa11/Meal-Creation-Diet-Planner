Help

Welcome to the Meal Creation Diet Planner! This system creates random meals from random ingredients, so don't be surprised if you find a first of its own combination, you can always regenerate the day you don't like, Here's everything you need to know to fill out the calculator and make the most of your meal plan:

Weight

Required - Enter your weight in kilograms (kg). You can use decimal numbers. Example: 70.5

Height

Required - Enter your height in centimeters (cm). Example: 175

Age

Required - Enter your age in years. Just numbers, like 22

Gender

Required - Choose either Male or Female from the dropdown.

Activity Level

Required - Use the slider to select how active you are:

  • 1 - Very Light: little to no exercise, office job
  • 2 - Light: occasional light activity, a somewhat active lifestyle
  • 3 - Moderate: moderate activity a few times a week, exersising 3 times a week
  • 4 - Heavy: daily intense workouts or a physically demanding job
  • 5 - Extremely Active: athlete or very high daily activity, a marathon runner is suitable

Goal

Required - Click to choose one goal. It helps personalize your plan:

  • Maintain Weight
  • Maintain Muscle Mass
  • Lose Fat
  • Build Muscle
  • Athlete

Diet Type

Required - Pick one of the following that fits your preference:

  • Vegan
  • Vegetarian
  • Pescatarian
  • Omnivore

Allergies & Dislikes (Optional)

List food items you can't eat, separated by commas. Example: milk, cheese, mushrooms. Avoid general terms like "lactose intolerance" — stick to food names. Any word you write here will be excluded from the meal plan if it appears in the database.

Meal Variety (Optional)

This slider decides how creative your meals get, it goes from 0% to 100%:

  • 0% - Traditional meal types (breakfast for breakfast, etc.)
  • 50% - Some crossover
  • 100% - Expect mixes like breakfast items in lunch! Its completely to have breakfast for dinner

Note: "None" doesn't mean boring - just conventional combinations.

Calculate and Generate

Clicking this button will:

  1. Show your daily nutritional requirements
  2. Generate a 7-day personalized meal plan

Meal Plan Features

  • You can regenerate any day if you don't like the meals.
  • Each meal has a color hue indicating the dominant macronutrient:
    • Red = Protein
    • Teal = Carbs
    • Blue = Fats
  • Hover over food items to view detailed nutritional info (if tooltips are enabled).

Logged-in Users

  • Your last entered data will appear automatically next time you log in.
  • Your most recent meal plan is also loaded when you sign in.

Guest Users

Your data is not saved permanently. If you refresh the page or leave, you'll need to re-enter your details.

Tip

Your meal plan appears in the "Diet Plan" tab once generated. You can always use the sidebar to switch between sections!
